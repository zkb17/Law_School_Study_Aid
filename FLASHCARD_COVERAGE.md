## Card methodology

- The 700 new cards were built from the same 700 original subject-specific concept/authority seeds as the expanded MCQ bank.
- Each course receives five name-to-rule recall prompts and five description-to-doctrine recognition prompts, plus all previous cards.
- Card IDs are stable; the release refreshes stale card queues while preserving saved self-assessment ratings and custom uploads.
- A legal authority reference accompanies every new card. Federal law, state law, and legal standards may evolve, and some citations are research starting points rather than verified pinpoint support.
- Study cards are original educational summaries and have not been individually reviewed by faculty.
