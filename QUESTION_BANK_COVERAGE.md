## Scope and provenance

- Questions are original: concept/application prompts authored from general legal doctrine, legal citations, and primary/secondary research resources. No commercial practice-bank questions were copied.
- Per-option explanations state the correct principle and distinguish nearby genuine alternatives.
- Rule citations accompany every addition; `sourceUrl` links to a targeted governing section when possible, or a related source directory/reference where a pinpoint link was not established.
- References consulted include current federal court rules, the U.S. Code, ABA model rules, the UCC, official patent examination references, and Cornell LII/Wex.
- Do not interpret a link alone as legal fact-checking of that question. A faculty reviewer should verify accuracy and jurisdiction-specific differences before treating this as an exam-authoritative resource.
- Most new additions test identification and conceptual distinction; they are not a replacement for advanced multi-issue fact-pattern exams.

## Mechanical validation

- 700 new entries; 70 subjects; 10 new entries per subject.
- No duplicate new question IDs or exact question stems.
- Exactly four distinct non-fictional legal concept options per question.
- Answer index always in range 0–3; correct option keyed by matching authored concept.
- Four option-specific explanations per question.
- Every new question has a named authority and HTTPS study reference.
- Existing 104 question IDs and saved progress history are not rewritten.
